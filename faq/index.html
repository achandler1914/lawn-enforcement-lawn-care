FAQ

Answers for common lawn care questions.

Find quick answers about service areas, quote requests, cleanups, recurring mowing, mulch, and what details help with pricing.

What areas do you serve?

Lawn Enforcement Lawn Care serves Portsmouth, Norfolk, Suffolk, Chesapeake, Virginia Beach, Newport News, and Hampton.

How do I request a quote?

Use the Request a Quote form or call 757-598-1499. Include your address, preferred service, and any photos or details that help describe the work.

Do you offer one-time cleanups?

Yes. Curb appeal cleanups, seasonal cleanups, leaf removal, bed cleanout, and property refresh services can be requested as one-time jobs.

Can I request recurring mowing?

Yes. Customers can request routine lawn mowing with trimming, edging, and blowing based on their property needs and schedule availability.

Do you install mulch?

Yes. Mulch installation is available for landscape beds and can be paired with bed cleanout or hedge trimming.

What should I include in a quote request?

Share the property address, service area, type of work needed, lawn size if known, timing, and the best way to reach you.
